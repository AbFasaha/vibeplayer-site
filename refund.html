Refund Policy

VibePlayer · Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Contact: zama27@duck.com

This page explains how to ask for a refund for VibePlayer Premium.

1. Direct-download purchases (Paystack)

If you paid for Premium for the direct-download version and want a refund, email zama27@duck.com and include:

  • The email address you used to pay.
  • Your Paystack payment reference.
  • The plan you bought (Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime) and the reason for the request.

We review each request individually and reply by email. A refund is not automatic and we do not promise a fixed refund window.

2. If a refund is approved

  • The Premium access given by that payment is removed.
  • The money is returned to the original payment method through the payment provider. How long it takes to appear depends on the provider and your bank.

3. Payment problems

If you were charged but Premium did not turn on, or you were charged twice, email us with your payment reference. We will check the payment and either activate Premium or refund the extra charge.

4. Google Play purchases

Once VibePlayer is available on Google Play, purchases made there are refunded according to Google Play’s refund policy, through Google, not through us.

5. Your rights

Nothing on this page limits any right you have under law.
