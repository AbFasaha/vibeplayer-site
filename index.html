What is VibePlayer?

VibePlayer is an Android app for playing your own media. It finds the songs and videos stored on your device, plays them with a modern player, and adds tools you would otherwise need several apps for. Your files stay on your phone: VibePlayer does not upload them.

For music

Browse songs, albums, artists and folders. Make playlists, mark favourites, and use the equalizer and sleep timer.

For video

A full-screen player with gesture controls, subtitles, playback speed, screen-ratio options and picture-in-picture.

For privacy

Move chosen songs and videos into a Private space protected by a PIN or your fingerprint.

Features

Everything below is in the app today.

Music library

Find all the songs on your device and browse by songs, albums, artists or folders. Favourites and playlists keep your best tracks close.

Video player

Gesture controls for brightness, volume and seeking, external subtitles (.srt, .vtt, .ass), and playback speed from 0.5× to 2× on the Free plan.

Screen ratio

Choose Best fit, Fill, Original, Stretch or classic ratios such as 16:9 and 4:3, and let the app remember your choice.

Pick up where you left off

Close the app and come back: your last song, queue and position are restored, and videos remember where you stopped.

Equalizer & sleep timer

Equalizer presets and a bass boost switch for everyone. Fall asleep to music with the sleep timer.

Private space

Hide selected songs and videos behind a PIN or fingerprint. Hidden files are moved into the app, so other apps cannot see them.

Built-in browser

Browse the web inside VibePlayer, with tabs, bookmarks and private tabs.

Download manager

Save media you own or have permission to download. VibePlayer never bypasses DRM, paywalls or sign-in restrictions.

Picture-in-picture

Keep a video playing in a small window while you use other apps.
