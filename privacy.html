Privacy Policy

VibePlayer · Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Contact: zama27@duck.com

This policy explains what information the VibePlayer Android app and this website handle. VibePlayer is developed by AbFasaha. You can reach us at zama27@duck.com.

1. This website

This website is a static information page. It has no accounts, no forms, no comments, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not set cookies of its own. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and we use it only to reply.

The website is delivered by a hosting provider, which may process technical data such as your IP address to serve the pages, under its own privacy terms.

2. The VibePlayer app

Your media

VibePlayer asks for permission to read audio and video on your device so it can list and play it. Your files are not uploaded by VibePlayer.

Information kept on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your device only: your play history, favourites and playlists; your last queue and playback position; video resume positions and screen-ratio choices; your settings and theme; browser history, bookmarks and download records; and your Private space settings. If you use the Private space, your PIN is not stored in readable form (only a salted, one-way hash), and files you move into Private are kept in the app’s own storage on your phone.

If the app crashes, it writes a crash report to your device. It is shared only if you choose to share it from Settings, and you can delete it there.

You can remove this information by clearing the app’s data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Doing this also deletes files you moved into the Private space.

Internet use

The app uses the internet for the built-in browser, the download manager, ads in the Free version, and purchases. When you browse a website or download a file, that website or server receives the usual request information, as with any browser or download tool.

Ads (Free version)

The Free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers and usage information to show ads, as described in Google’s advertising policies. Where required, the app shows Google’s consent form first, and you can review your choice later in Settings. Premium removes ads.

Payments

Purchases made through Google Play are handled by Google Play Billing; we do not receive your card details. Purchases made for the direct-download version are processed by Paystack, which handles your payment details directly. We receive the payment status, the payment reference and the email address used for the payment, so we can confirm your purchase and help you with support.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not upload your music or videos.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Children

VibePlayer is a general-purpose media player and is not designed for children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

5. Your choices

  • Withdraw or change ad consent in the app’s Settings.
  • Delete data stored by the app by clearing its data or uninstalling it.
  • Ask us what information we hold about you (for example, payment records and emails) and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing zama27@duck.com. We may need to keep some payment records where the law or the payment provider requires it.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy: zama27@duck.com.
